I am an AI Research Intern at Samsung Research America in Mountain View, and a researcher at Korea University’s DMIS Lab, advised by Prof. Jaewoo Kang.

My research focuses on memory systems for AI agents and visually grounded reasoning. I study how agents can organize experience, recall the right evidence, and learn to make better decisions.
